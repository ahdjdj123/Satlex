## Overview

**SATLEX** is an educational platform designed to make SAT vocabulary acquisition more structured, contextual, and effective.

Rather than encouraging students to memorise isolated definitions, SATLEX aims to develop a deeper understanding of vocabulary through contextual explanations, synonyms, examples, difficulty progression, and active learning.

Its central philosophy is simple:

> Learning a word is not the same as understanding how to use it.

SATLEX brings vocabulary learning into a more organised digital environment, helping students develop the linguistic precision required for the SAT Reading and Writing section.

## Core Features

### 📚 Contextual Vocabulary Learning
- Clear and comprehensive word definitions.
- Contextual explanations to understand meaning in different situations.
- Synonyms and related expressions.
- Practical example sentences.
- Phonetic information and pronunciation support.

### 🎯 Structured Difficulty Progression
Vocabulary organised into progressive difficulty levels:

- Foundation
- Intermediate
- Advanced
- Highly Advanced

This structure helps learners gradually expand their vocabulary without being overwhelmed by unfamiliar terminology.

### 🧠 Active Learning
A learning-oriented environment designed to encourage:
- Vocabulary challenges.
- Knowledge reinforcement.
- Recognition of commonly confused words.
- Progress tracking and vocabulary mastery.

### 📈 Personal Vocabulary Development
A structured approach to individual learning, with planned support for:
- Favourite words.
- Personal notes.
- Review history.
- Learning progress.
- Individual vocabulary collections.

### 🎨 Modern User Interface
SATLEX uses a refined navy-and-blue visual identity, combining a clean interface with a focused educational experience.

The design prioritises:
- Readability.
- Clear information hierarchy.
- Minimal visual distractions.
- Consistent navigation.
- A cohesive learning environment.

## Educational Philosophy

SATLEX is built around three fundamental principles:

**1. Context over memorisation**

Understanding how a word functions in a sentence is more valuable than memorising a definition without context.

**2. Progression over information overload**

Vocabulary should be introduced through a structured difficulty system that encourages gradual development.

**3. Active recall over passive reading**

Students should regularly retrieve and apply previously learned vocabulary instead of simply reviewing word lists.

## Project Vision

SATLEX aims to become more than a vocabulary dictionary.

Its long-term direction is to create a comprehensive SAT vocabulary ecosystem in which students can discover, understand, practise, organise, and retain academic vocabulary.

The ultimate objective is to make vocabulary acquisition more systematic and accessible.
